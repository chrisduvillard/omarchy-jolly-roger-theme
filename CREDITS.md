# Credits

Jolly Roger ships two kinds of wallpaper: original artwork made for the theme,
and paintings, charts and illustrations that are in the public domain.

## Original artwork

Painted procedurally for this theme (JavaScript on an HTML canvas; the source
is in [`art/`](art/)) and released under the [MIT licence](LICENSE) with the
rest of the repository.

## Public-domain works

Every work below was made by an artist who died more than a century ago, and is
in the public domain. The reproductions come from Wikimedia Commons, which
hosts them as public-domain or CC0 files. Each was cropped to 16:9 and resized;
nothing was repainted. `art/paintings.tsv` records the exact source file and
crop for each one, and `art/paintings.sh --fetch` rebuilds them.
